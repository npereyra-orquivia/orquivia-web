Cookies policy.

Information about cookies and similar technologies used on the Orquivia website.

Current status No analytics or advertising

The website only remembers your cookie choice and whether the initial logo animation was shown recently.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files or similar technologies that may be stored on a user's device when visiting a website. They may be used to make a website work correctly, remember preferences or measure usage.

2. Cookies and local storage used by this website

At this time, Orquivia does not use analytics, advertising or commercial tracking cookies. The site may use browser local storage to remember whether the user accepted or rejected the cookie notice, avoiding showing it on every visit. This preference is not used for profiling or commercial tracking.

  • Name: orquiviaCookieConsent
  • Type: local preference / technical
  • Purpose: remember the cookie notice choice
  • Provider: Orquivia
  • Retention: until the browser preference is deleted

The website also uses local storage to remember whether the initial logo animation has already been shown for a limited period, solely to improve browsing experience.

  • Name: orquiviaLoaderSeenAt
  • Type: local preference / user experience
  • Purpose: avoid repeating the initial animation on every navigation
  • Provider: Orquivia
  • Retention: approximately 8 hours or until browser data is deleted

3. Third-party technologies

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts to maintain its visual identity. This may involve a connection to Google servers to deliver font files. Orquivia does not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels or tracking tools in this version of the website.

If analytical, advertising, non-technical personalization cookies or any other technology requiring consent are added in the future, this policy will be updated and prior consent will be requested where necessary.

5. Deleting cookies or local storage

You may also delete cookies and website data from your browser settings. The process depends on the browser used, but is usually found under privacy, security, history or browsing data settings.

If website data is deleted, the cookie notice or initial animation may be shown again on a later visit.

6. Updates

This policy may be updated if new tools, cookies or similar technologies are incorporated. The current version will be the one published on this page.
